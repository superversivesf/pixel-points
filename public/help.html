PIXEL POINTS

HOW TO PLAY

1. Get everyone in

The Scrum Master creates a room and shares the 4-letter code (shown big on their screen). Everyone else joins with the code and a display name. No accounts, no sign-ups — Jackbox style.

2. Describe the story

The Scrum Master types a one-sentence description of the story you're estimating. It appears at the top of every player's screen for the whole round, so nobody loses the thread.

3. Play your card

Each player holds a private hand of cards: 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 (Fibonacci story points) plus two specials:

  • ☕ COFFEE — the story is too big / unclear, or you need a break.
  • ❓ ? — you need more information before you can estimate.

Tap a card to vote. You can change your mind any time until the reveal — just tap a different card.

4. The reveal

When every player has cast a vote, a 5-second countdown starts (any vote change resets it). When it hits zero, all hands are revealed at once — nobody can be influenced by anyone else's card. The Scrum Master shares their screen on a call so everyone sees the big board: each card, the spread (lowest to highest), and a suggested middle value.

5. Decide together

Now the team talks it through. The Scrum Master picks one of:

  • CONSENSUS — record the agreed points for the story. It's saved to the session history.
  • RE VOTE — same story, new round, cards cleared.
  • NEW ROUND — drop it and move to the next story.

A big spread (e.g. 3 vs 21) usually means the story needs a conversation before a re-vote. Coffee and Question cards never count towards points.

Good to know

  • Phone dropped mid-vote? Your card still counts, and you auto-rejoin when you're back.
  • Refresh the page — you're put straight back in your seat.
  • Rooms die after 15 empty minutes; history lives only as long as the room.
  • Six themes available in the header — including two readable Mono themes.
